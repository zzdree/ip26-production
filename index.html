⚡ Standard Operating Procedure & Safety Matrix

FIELD RIGGING PROTOCOLS

🔌 1. AC Power Distribution & Load Balancing

  • All main switcher power supplies and distribution splitters must be routed to isolated AC phases.
  • No heavy chaining of ungrounded 2-pin adapters on primary mixer strips.
  • Operator workstations (OBS, Resolume, ProPresenter) must run on dedicated grounded surge protection strips.

🎥 2. High-Speed Video Links & RF Hollyland

  • Relieve mechanical tension on camera Micro-HDMI ports using 30cm strain relief breakout cables.
  • Hollyland Pyro H 5.8GHz TX antenna must maintain clean line-of-sight elevation over the audience.
  • Long-distance HDMI runs (20M/30M) must be taped flat along auditorium floor perimeters.

📦 3. Strike & Teardown Protocol (Usung-Usung)

  • Power off video processors and audio submixers before disengaging balanced lines.
  • Roll all HDMI and XLR lines using the Over-Under technique to protect copper cores.
  • Verify physical return of all loan gear against the Equipment Depot tab before exiting the venue.
